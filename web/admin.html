Payroll & salary

These rules are saved and used by Payroll. Attendance hours stay elapsed time; this module converts them into pay.

Leave

Attendance deductions

One day is the monthly salary divided by the divisor. A 6:00 shift clock-in at 6:15:00 is still on time.

  • 16–30 minutes late: 0.25 day
  • 31–60 minutes late: 0.50 day
  • More than 60 minutes late: 1 day
  • No clock-out: 0.5 day
  • No clock-in: 1 day
  • Absent without permission: 2 days
